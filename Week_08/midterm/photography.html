Photography

For my third semester at McHenry County College I am learning Photography

This Series is Called,

"Heart of the Midwest"

  1. I really wanted to convey the small town I grew up in as more than vacant
  2. I also wanted to portray the closeness of being with my family using Tension Framing
  3. Tension Framing is a framing technique that deliberately creates a sense of unease, anticipation, or emotional resonance
  4. I wanted to convey something warm and cozy by bringing everything close to the camera
  5. A lot of my art is strongly connected to music, this series sounds like Green isn't Yellow by All-American Rejects

Stay Tuned for my Next Series,

"The Bond of Music"
