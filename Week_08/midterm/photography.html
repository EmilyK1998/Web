Photography

For my third semester at McHenry County College I am learning Photography

    This series is called "Heart of the Midwest

  1. I really wanted to convey the small town I grew up in as more than vacant
  2. I also wanted to portray the closeness of being with my family using Tension Framing
      Tension Framing is a framing technique that deliberately creates a sense of unease, anticipation, or emotional resonance
  3. I wanted to convey something warm and cozy by bringing everything close to the camera
