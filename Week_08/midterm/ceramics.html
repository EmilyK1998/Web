Let's Talk Techniques

  1. Pinch Pots
      Hollowing out a soft piece of clay with the thumb to build a form
      One of the oldest techniques of ceramics
  2. Coil Pots
      Using rolled coils of clay, scoring them together using slip to build a form
      Slipis extremely watered down clay used as "glue" to hold clay together
      Scoring making scratch marks with a clay tool all over the clay bodies you want to stick together with slip.
  3. The Wheel
      A fast turning table that allows you to use friction to build a form NOT as easy as it looks!
